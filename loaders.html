CategoryRegion SeverityCount Note
Organisation nameinternational low 2Not personal data on its own. Reported because organisation names are often what makes an otherwise anonymous document re-identifiable.
Date of birthinternational high 1Special-risk identifier in combination with a name. Only dates near an explicit birth-date label are reported, so an unlabelled DOB is missed.
National Insurance numberUK high 1A direct identifier under UK data protection law. Uniquely identifies an individual and is routinely treated as high risk in a breach assessment.
Email addressinternational medium 1Personal data whenever it identifies an individual, including firstname.lastname@ addresses at a corporate domain.
Phone numberUK medium 1Personal data when tied to an individual. UK formats only; international numbers other than +44 are not detected.
Street addressUK medium 1Detected by a house-number-plus-street-type heuristic. It will miss flats, named houses and multi-line addresses split across lines.
UK postcodeUK medium 1A full postcode narrows location to a handful of properties and is treated as personal data when combined with any other identifier.
UK sort codeUK high 1Financial identifier. On its own it identifies a bank branch; combined with an account number it enables direct debit fraud.
UK bank account numberUK high 1Financial identifier. Eight bare digits are far too common to report without a nearby label, so an unlabelled account number will not be found.
IBANinternational high 1Financial identifier, validated here with the ISO 13616 mod-97 checksum, so false positives are rare.
Payment card numberinternational high 1Payment card data. Validated with the Luhn checksum and the issuing network's prefix and length. Storing this in unencrypted documents is a PCI DSS problem as well as a data protection one.
UK VAT numberUK low 1Business identifier rather than personal data, unless the business is a sole trader, in which case it is personal data.
Unique Taxpayer ReferenceUK high 1HMRC taxpayer identifier. Ten bare digits are too common to report without a nearby label.
Person nameinternational medium 1Found by a local statistical model, not a rule. Expect both misses and false positives; treat the count as an indication of scale, not an inventory.
