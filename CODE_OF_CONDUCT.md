# Code of conduct

complydoc follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not the
person. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated, in the repository, in discussions or
anywhere the project is represented.

To report behaviour that breaks it, write to hi@complydoc.io. Reports are read only by
the maintainers and kept confidential. The maintainers may remove comments, close
threads, or block anyone who breaks it, as the covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
